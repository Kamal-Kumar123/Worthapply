# Problem Definition

## The Problem

Students searching for internships and jobs face a time-intensive
evaluation bottleneck. Before applying to each opportunity, they need to:

1. **Verify** — Is this posting current? Is the company real?
2. **Match** — Do my skills meet the requirements?
3. **Assess** — Are there risk indicators I should be aware of?
4. **Prioritize** — Should I spend 30-60 minutes on this application?

## Who Has This Problem?

University students and recent graduates actively searching for
internships and entry-level positions. They typically evaluate
10-30 opportunities per week during active job search periods.

## The Bottleneck

**Manual investigation time per opportunity: 15-45 minutes.**

This includes reading the full job description, cross-referencing the
company, mapping skills to requirements, and making a subjective
judgment about whether to apply.

## Why It Matters

A student's time during job search is their most constrained resource.
Without efficient triage:

- **Burnout**: Students stop applying after exhausting their patience
- **Wasted time**: Applications to poor-fit or questionable opportunities
- **Missed opportunities**: Legitimate good-fit roles overlooked due to fatigue
- **Poor decisions**: Lack of structured evaluation leads to gut-feel prioritization

## What WorthApply Does

Given a student's resume and a job URL/description, WorthApply produces
a structured **Opportunity Intelligence Report** that separates:

- Student-job fit (with evidence)
- Opportunity confidence (verification status)
- Risk indicators (signals, not accusations)
- Evidence quality
- Actionable recommendation

## What WorthApply Does NOT Do

- Submit applications
- Send emails or contact recruiters
- Make consequential decisions
- Claim to detect fraud
- Replace human judgment

WorthApply provides **decision support**. The student always makes
the final decision.
